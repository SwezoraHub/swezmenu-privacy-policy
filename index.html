SwezMenu Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

SwezMenu respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the SwezMenu app, how that information is used, and the third-party services that may process information in connection with the app.

1. Information You Provide

SwezMenu allows you to take a photo of a restaurant menu using your device camera or select a menu image from your device gallery. The selected image is used to provide the menu analysis feature.

Menu images may be transmitted to our server for processing and analysis. We do not use menu images to identify you personally. Please avoid submitting images containing unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

2. Camera and Photos

The app may request access to your camera when you choose to take a menu photo. The app may also access a photo that you explicitly choose from your device gallery. These permissions are used to provide the menu scanning and analysis functionality.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

SwezMenu stores certain app preferences locally on your device. These may include settings such as your vibration preference and whether the Remove Ads purchase has been restored or recognized.

This information is stored locally and is not used to create a personal profile.

4. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

SwezMenu uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may automatically process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for purposes including advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Information handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be transmitted securely using encryption in transit.

You can manage advertising and advertising identifier settings through the controls available on your Android device. When you successfully purchase the Remove Ads feature, the app stops displaying its advertisements.

5. Purchases and Google Play Billing

SwezMenu offers a Remove Ads purchase through Google Play Billing. Payments and payment information are handled by Google Play and its applicable payment systems.

SwezMenu does not directly collect or store your payment card number, bank account details, or other payment credentials.

The app may receive purchase status information from Google Play in order to recognize a successful or restored Remove Ads purchase and enable the corresponding feature.

6. Third-Party Services

SwezMenu may use third-party services to provide app functionality, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising and Google Play Billing for in-app purchases.

Menu images may also be transmitted to the service used by SwezMenu to perform menu analysis.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed by SwezMenu is used to:

  • provide menu image analysis;
  • provide app features and functionality;
  • remember selected app preferences;
  • recognize and maintain the Remove Ads purchase status; and
  • display and support advertisements through AdMob.

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. Data transmitted by supported third-party services may be protected using encryption in transit.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

SwezMenu does not require you to create an account. App preference data stored locally can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app, subject to the behavior of the Android operating system and applicable third-party services.

Menu images transmitted for analysis may be processed by the service providing the analysis functionality. Retention of data processed by third-party services may be governed by their own policies and retention practices.

10. Children's Privacy

SwezMenu is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information through the app.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, or its data practices change. Any updated version will be made available through the app and the public Privacy Policy webpage.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding privacy or data handling, please contact the developer through the support contact provided on the Google Play listing for SwezMenu.
